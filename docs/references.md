# References

Looked at, never copied. A reference is where a fact comes from or what a drawing was compared with.

- **Uniform sections of Wikipedia articles** (text, CC BY-SA): the colours and stripe layouts of each team's uniforms, recorded here as data. No picture from Wikimedia Commons is used or copied.
- **nhluniforms.com** era pages, and the **kit diagrams of the national ice hockey teams on Wikimedia Commons** (CC BY-SA): looked at to read where the stripes of an NHL or national hockey jersey sit and how wide they are. Looked at only; nothing from them is copied or traced.
- **Wikidata** (CC0): team names and colours.
- **Clubs' published colour codes and brand pages** where they exist.
- A poster of NHL jerseys from 1905 to 2015 (barber-pole hoops, the early and defunct teams): looked at for what to cover.
- [Best NHL jerseys of all time](https://starrcards.com/best-nhl-jerseys-of-all-time-headlined-by-blackhawks-canadiens-and-kings/) (Starr Cards): looked at for which looks are remembered.
- WCAG 2.2, [contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum): the 4.5 to 1 ratio the numbers and names are held to.

A preset records a layout it could read from a source; where a source gave only colour names, the layout is the plainest one that fits, and the row is marked `unsure` in its source file.
